Learning Objectives

By completing this lab, you should be able to:

  • Recognize common indicators associated with {{ attack.name }}.
  • Analyze a simulated security scenario.
  • Identify appropriate defensive measures.
  • Explain how to report and document a suspected threat.

Safety Guidelines

Follow these rules throughout the exercise.

  • Use fictional scenarios and authorized training environments.
  • Do not test systems, accounts, or networks without permission.
  • Never enter real passwords, personal information, or confidential data into practice exercises.
  • Focus on identifying, preventing, and responding to threats.

HANDS-ON PRACTICE

Lab Tasks

Work through each task and mark it complete when finished.

Task 1: Identify Indicators

Observe the scenario and identify possible warning signs.

Review the learning material for {{ attack.name }}. Identify at least three warning signs that could indicate suspicious or malicious activity.

Expected outcome: A list of relevant indicators and a brief explanation of why each one matters.

Task 2: Analyze the Scenario

Consider the possible risks and consequences.

Imagine that a user reports activity potentially associated with {{ attack.name }}. Review the available information and consider which assets, accounts, or data might be at risk.

Task 3: Recommend Defenses

Choose suitable protective measures.

Based on your findings, recommend practical steps that could prevent the attack, reduce its impact, or help detect similar activity in the future.
